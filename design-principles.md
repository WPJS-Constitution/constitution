# Project-wide Design Principles

These principles explain the intentions behind the developing Constitution. They guide Topic drafting but are not themselves constitutional clauses unless adopted as clauses in a Topic.

## Deliberate governance

> Governance is not a Thoroughbred; it is a Clydesdale.

This is the project's wording for a deliberate approach to governance: make room for discussion, participation, and disciplined consideration rather than treating speed as success.

Its current applications include:

- Meetings provide space for debate and discussion; they do not decide matters or hold votes.
- Decisions are made through electronic voting, which stays open while votes still outstanding could change the result.
- The process does not create artificial voting deadlines or emergency routes for pushing decisions through.
- A **Needs Work** vote gives members a way to call for change and requires a reason to be shown on the voting dashboard.

These applications are developing in the Meetings and Voting Topics and may be refined as those Topics evolve.
