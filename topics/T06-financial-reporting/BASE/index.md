# T06 — Financial Reporting

**Topic ID:** T06  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposal for discussion; not formally approved

## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the first independently developed starting point for Financial Reporting. It defines what WPJS reports, when it reports, and what officials and members should be able to understand from those reports.

The Finances Topic remains authoritative for the financial year, handling of money, records, fees, and spending. This Topic defines the reporting outputs and refers to those controls rather than replacing them.

No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous Constitution.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- The financial year ends on the last day of February under T04-C01. The proposed quarterly periods therefore run March to May, June to August, September to November, and December to February.
- The five required reports are four quarterly reports and one Annual Financial Report.
- Officials need the exact financial position and figures, down to the final cent. Members need a clear account of financial health, income, and expenditure.
- The guiding principle is “every cent accounted for.”
- The Annual Financial Report includes the detailed budget-versus-actual comparison. The dashboard gives members a summary of budgets, results, and an indicator of how well each budget anticipated actual income and expenditure.
- The indicator’s method is defined by Policy and the accountant sets the score. The figures and basis remain visible; the indicator does not replace them.
- The draft Annual Financial Report is sent with the AGM notice and finalised at the AGM using the final financial information and any adjustments made there. Meetings do not vote.
- T06-C10 records the current audit intention only. Its wording and scope must be refined after consultation with accounting officials.
- Membership fees contribute to WPJS’s costs, including fixed costs such as external audit and reliable, monitored email delivery. Fee setting and budgeting remain under T04; this Topic reports the resulting income and expenditure. No fee is presumed to be ring-fenced for a particular cost.
- Policy may describe reporting methods, but must not weaken or waive requirements in this Topic.

## Open design questions

- The exact dates by which quarterly reports are due, to be committed to by the accountant before those dates are published on the dashboard.
- The Policy-defined method for the budget indicator and how the accountant sets the score.
- The scope and wording of the external audit requirement after consultation with accounting officials.
- The minimum detail and format for the year-end financial position and the Annual Financial Report, provided the exact figures and required comparisons remain clear.
- How corrected reports and prior versions are retained and displayed.

These questions do not prevent this BASE from recording the agreed starting principles. They must be resolved before the Topic is closed for a Constitution edition.
