# T08 — Authority, Accountability and Independent Review

**Topic ID:** T08  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposal for discussion; not formally approved

## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the starting principles for where authority within WPJS comes from, how it is assigned and used, how office holders remain answerable to the Constituency, and how decisions can be challenged independently.

The clauses reflect the agreed principle that office holders serve the Constituency and the community WPJS exists to support. Holding a title does not create personal or unlimited power. Authority must come from this Constitution or be validly delegated under it.

No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous Constitution.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- The Constitution and its member-defined processes provide WPJS's internal authority, subject to applicable law.
- T01-C01 states whom WPJS exists to serve. This Topic distinguishes that wider community from the formal Constituency that holds member decision rights.
- Parents and families make an important contribution to sustaining junior squash, including financially; this does not by itself define membership or voting rights.
- The Constituency's powers, the authority delegated to Governance roles, and the authority delegated to Operations roles must be explicit.
- A small elected Representative Council that selects an Operations Team from nominations is a model Rob has expressed interest in. It remains a proposal to test, not an agreed structure or a requirement imposed by King V.
- T05-C08 establishes that Governance and Operations are separate functions. T08-C08 describes the oversight boundary between them.
- The Membership Topic will define eligible individual and institutional members, including the process by which an institution authorises a representative to cast its one vote and demonstrates how it reached that position.
- A person may be an individual member and also be authorised to cast a separate vote for an institutional member. Those ballots are attached to distinct member entities, not duplicate personal memberships.
- T08-C01 was retired because its statement of who WPJS serves duplicated T01-C01. Its clause ID is not reused.
- Independent review is intended to ensure WPJS is not the final arbitrator of its own decisions. The reviewer’s appointment, process, powers, and any further appeal route remain open.
- Requirements for recording significant decisions must support transparency without turning routine operational steps into unnecessary administration.
- Policy may explain implementation but must not weaken or remove requirements in this Topic.

## Open design questions

- Which decisions are reserved to the Constituency, which are delegated to Governance, and which are delegated to Operations.
- How governing roles are elected, how operational roles are selected, and what powers each group holds.
- How the Constituency may initiate accountability or removal of a role holder, including one selected rather than directly elected.
- Who hears an independent review, how independence is secured, and what outcomes the reviewer may order.
- Which decisions are significant enough to require a record, and how those records are made available while protecting privacy and children.

These questions do not prevent this BASE from recording the agreed starting principles. They must be resolved before the Topic is closed for a Constitution edition.
