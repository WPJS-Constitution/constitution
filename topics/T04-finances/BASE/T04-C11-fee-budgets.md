# T04-C11 — Fees must be based on a budget

Before WPJS sets or charges any fee, it must prepare a budget showing the expected costs and income and how the fee was calculated. This applies to every fee, including fees for tournaments, programmes, coaching, and other services. A fee must not be set by guesswork. A member’s refusal to pay an unbudgeted fee must not prevent a player from taking part in the related activity. This fee-setting budget does not create an overall Operations budget or restrict Operations’ authority to run WPJS.
