## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the first independently developed starting point for the Finances topic. It is intended to protect WPJS’s funds while allowing Operations to carry out the work needed to keep junior squash running.

No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous Constitution.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- Operations does not require an approved operating budget. Its spending authority is guided by the funds available and the likely costs of continuing its work.
- The 5% threshold is a trigger for discussion of a proposed payment or commitment, not a fixed rand cap or automatic spending approval or prohibition.
- The 48-hour period for recording and accounting for bank transactions begins when the relevant bank statement becomes available. Policy determines how and when statements are obtained.
- The financial dashboard is available to every member through their subscription.
- A budget must support every fee, including tournament fees. This fee-setting budget does not create an Operations budget.
- If the Treasurer is removed for misuse of funds, the Chair is automatically removed because the Chair is responsible for oversight, regardless of personal involvement.
- The Finance Policy must explain how the 5% threshold is applied, including how related payments are treated so that a commitment is not split to avoid discussion.
- Policy may describe implementation, but must not weaken or waive requirements in this Topic.

## Open design questions

- How the 5% trigger is calculated against the money held in WPJS’s bank accounts at the time of a proposed commitment.
- How a discussion about non-operational spending proceeds and when it requires an electronic vote.
- How members access the dashboard through their subscription, and how its displayed figures are dated and refreshed.
- What detail and supporting material must appear in a budget for each fee.
- How the external company conducting the annual audit is selected and how the audit outcome is reported.
- What safeguards and recovery practices support transferable digital records and multiple-person access.
- How the Operations function is defined and assigned, given that the Constitution defines functions rather than assuming traditional committee positions.

These questions do not prevent this BASE from recording the agreed starting principles. They must be resolved before the Topic is closed for a Constitution edition.
