# T04-C09 — Responsibility for funds

The official responsible for WPJS funds is accountable for safeguarding and properly managing them, including money received, payments made, and how the funds are used. Keeping complete records is part of that responsibility. The official must be able to account for all funds under their responsibility. An unexplained transaction, missing supporting record, or failure to account for funds is enough to trigger a disciplinary process. The official must account for the funds; members do not have to prove misuse before the process can begin.
