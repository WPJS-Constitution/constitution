## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the first independently developed starting point for the Intention topic. It captures the philosophy that WPJS governance should make practical progress possible through clear guidance, accountable authority, deliberate decision-making, and proportionate safeguards.

No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous Constitution.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- T05-C02 states what the Constitution establishes: outcomes, responsibilities, and safeguards. T05-C03 explains how to read and apply it as guidance for carrying out WPJS duties, not as a step-by-step operational script.
- Operations and governance must be performed by separate groups. Later Topics must define the functions and arrangements needed to make this separation work.
- The Constitution governs WPJS. External guidance may be considered, but does not become part of the Constitution unless WPJS adopts it through its own process and within applicable law.
- The authoritative text and clause histories are maintained only through the designated version-controlled repository. Other copies may be used for reading or discussion but do not amend the authoritative text.
- Deliberate discussion, participation, and discipline support sound decisions; speed alone is not a measure of good governance.
- Detailed voting and meeting mechanics remain in their respective Topics.

## Open design questions

- What functions and responsibilities distinguish the Operations group from the governance group, and how will the separation be maintained in practice?
- What counts as external guidance adopted by WPJS, and through which process?
- What supporting records, explanations, or evidence must accompany a proposed change to the Constitution?
- How will version-control and repository access be maintained if the people responsible change?

These questions do not prevent this BASE from recording the agreed starting principles. They must be resolved before the Topic is closed for a Constitution edition.
