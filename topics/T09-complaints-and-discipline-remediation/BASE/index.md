# T09 — Complaints and Discipline Remediation

**Topic ID:** T09  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposed

## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the agreed starting clauses for Remediation of disputed sanctions and Member Objections. It sets out how the parties can be heard, how WPJS records the outcome, and when a party may ask for a Disciplinary.
