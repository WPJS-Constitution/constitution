# T07 — Policies

**Topic ID:** T07  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposal for discussion; not formally approved

## Origin and intended outcome

This BASE records the starting principles for Policies: what they are for, who prepares and approves them, how members give them authority, how they relate to governance terms, and how their current and past versions remain accessible.

## Design notes

- T05-C08 establishes that Operations and governance are separate functions carried out by separate groups. This Topic assigns Policy proposal work and sign-off consistently with that separation.
- An SGM may be used for discussion. Policy decisions are made by electronic member vote, consistent with the Meetings and Voting Topics.
- Renewing an unchanged Policy is the agreed exception to the member-vote requirement for new, rewritten, or retired Policies.
- T07 is currently listed after T03 in the MRI. Its final place in an assembled Constitution remains open to review.

## Open design questions

- If a Policy is not renewed at the end of its stated term, does it expire automatically, or does it require a member vote to retire it? How does that work with T07-C10?
- What happens if a proposed rewrite is not approved before the current Policy’s term ends?
- What cloud-based location and version-history method should be used?
- Does “accessible to everyone” mean every stakeholder, or unrestricted public access?
- What details of drafting, governance sign-off, member votes, and renewals must be kept with each Policy record?

These questions do not prevent this BASE from recording the agreed starting principles. They must be resolved before the Topic is closed for a Constitution edition.
