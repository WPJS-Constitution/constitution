# T01 — Purpose, Role and Authority of the ccc

**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposal for discussion; not formally approved

## Origin and intended outcome

This is the first independently developed starting point for the new WPJS body. It was developed from the agreed purpose and design outcomes in discussion. No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous ccc.

The topic should make clear whom the ccc serves, what role it plays, how policies relate to it, and how people entrusted with junior squash responsibilities are expected to use their authority.

## Proposed clauses

### T01-C01 — Purpose and community served

The ccc exists to keep junior squash alive and sustainable in the Western Cape. It serves junior players and the wider junior squash community, including families, clubs, coaches, administrators, schools, and others who support junior squash.

### T01-C02 — Role of the ccc

The ccc is the guide for people entrusted with looking after junior squash. It provides a framework for keeping activities running and for making fair, transparent decisions about how they are run.

### T01-C03 — Policies and implementation

The ccc sets the lasting framework: what must be achieved, who is responsible, and how authority is held accountable. Policies explain how the work is done. A policy cannot override the ccc and must be reviewed when the people responsible for governance change.

### T01-C04 — Authority as service

People exercising responsibilities under the ccc do so to serve the junior squash community. Those responsibilities are not anyone’s personal property.

### T01-C05 — A new foundation

This ccc is being created for a new WPJS body. No wording, rule, structure, or decision from a previous constitution carries over automatically. Any similar provision must be included because it is independently chosen as right for this body.

### T01-C06 — Legal status and plain language

Once properly adopted, the ccc is WPJS’s formal governing document. It must operate within applicable South African law, including laws that protect children. It must be written in plain, understandable English, with any necessary technical terms explained.

## Design notes (not proposed ccc clauses)

- The ccc is being designed as a stand-alone framework for junior squash in the Western Cape. It should not refer to WPS or depend on WPS approval.
- WPS affiliation conditions are a separate external relationship matter. They do not automatically determine the ccc’s contents. Any provision that resembles external guidance must be chosen on its own merits for this body and community.
- Membership and voting rights, including who can formally amend the ccc, are reserved for their own topics.
- The exact legal form of the new WPJS body and the requirements for valid adoption need to be confirmed before this topic is closed.
