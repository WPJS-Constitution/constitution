# T01 — Purpose, Status and Role of the Constitution

**Topic ID:** T01  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposal for discussion; not formally approved

## Origin and intended outcome

This is the first independently developed starting point for the new WPJS body. It was developed from the agreed purpose and design outcomes in discussion. No wording, rule, structure, or decision is inherited from a previous Constitution.

This Topic makes clear whom the Constitution serves, its formal status, its role as the lasting framework for WPJS, and how Policies relate to it. T08 deals with the authority and accountability of people acting under the Constitution.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- The Constitution is being designed as a stand-alone framework for junior squash in the Western Cape. It should not refer to WPS or depend on WPS approval.
- WPS affiliation conditions are a separate external relationship matter. They do not automatically determine the Constitution’s contents. Any provision that resembles external guidance must be chosen on its own merits for this body and community.
- T01-C04 was retired because its general service principle is addressed more directly by T08-C04 and T08-C06. Its clause ID is not reused.
- Membership and voting rights, including who can formally amend the Constitution, are reserved for their own topics.
- The exact legal form of the new WPJS body and the requirements for valid adoption need to be confirmed before this topic is closed.
