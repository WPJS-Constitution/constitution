# T11 — Disciplinary

**Topic ID:** T11  
**Topic version:** BASE  
**Status:** Draft in progress; not closed for a Constitution edition  
**Approval status:** Proposed

## Origin and intended outcome

Set out when a party may ask for a Disciplinary after Remediation, how the panel is appointed, and how it reaches and records its ruling.

## Design notes (not proposed Constitution clauses)

- T11-C02 through T11-C05 cover panel appointment, independence and conflicts. T11-C07 through T11-C14 cover panel decision-making and written rulings.
- Disciplinary panel members serve without a fee under T11-C15.
- The Appointer is part of the [T12 Appeals process](../../T12-appeals/BASE/index.md).
- T11-C06 — Filling a vacant seat was retired. Its clause ID is reserved and is not reused.
- Clauses on how the Disciplinary hearing works are still to be drafted.
