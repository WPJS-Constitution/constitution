## How to use this index

- During drafting, update a Topic’s latest snapshot and status when a new BASE or numbered Topic snapshot is recorded.
- A Topic is closed when its discussion and drafting are complete, and it is closed for the next Constitution edition.
- Before assembling a new Constitution edition, bring every Topic to that edition number. Topics not newly dealt with are copied forward unchanged and marked as carried forward.
- The index for a particular Constitution edition lists the Topics only; the edition number makes their matching Topic version implicit.
- Do not treat a Topic as formally approved merely because it is closed or listed here. Record formal approval separately.
